## About Me

Pegawai Teknologi Maklumat Kanan at Seksyen Penyelaras, Unit Audit Dalam, Kementerian Pendidikan Tinggi (KPT) — and a full-stack developer who has shipped more than 20 working systems.

My work sits where ICT auditing meets software engineering. In the office I coordinate the unit's ICT audit work — audit meetings, annual reporting, MyPrestasi and MyPortfolio — and build the internal web systems that turn manual, paper-heavy processes into clean online workflows.

Before KPT, I spent five years at Unit Teknikal dan ICT, Cawangan Pentadbiran dan Kewangan, Bahagian Pengurusan Sumber Manusia (BPSM), Kementerian Pendidikan (KPM), leading the ICT technical unit and delivering the systems the division runs on every day.

Outside office hours I build SaaS products, AI tools and automation in Bahasa Melayu. Every web system I build is hosted on a subdomain of akmalmarvis.com.
